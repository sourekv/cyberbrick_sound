Engine sound sets

Sound sets for the cyberbrick_sound module: see what is on it, try one, change or add a set. Chrome or Edge on a computer, the module on USB (firmware engine_sets).

This page needs Chrome or Edge on a computer.
Not connected.
While the page talks to the module, the engine on the module is silent; it comes back a few seconds after the last action.
What a good sample is like (format, file names, lengths, loops)
  • Format on the module: WAV (.wav), PCM, 22 050 Hz, 16 bit, mono, with fixed names: name.wav, start.wav, horn.wav, shift.wav, and for a sample set idle.wav and loaded.wav, plus set.json with the parameters, all in the folder NN_description (NN = the number). This page writes all of that itself: you can drop WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC or M4A at any rate, stereo or mono, under any name; it is converted and renamed. Best source: WAV or FLAC at 44.1 or 48 kHz (MP3 adds its own noise).
  • Lengths: name 1-2 s, starter 0.5-2 s, horn 1-2 s, shift 0.1-1 s, idle loop 1-6 s, loaded loop 1-3 s. A set takes about 0.2-0.6 MB; the module has room for about 4.6 MB.
  • Loops (idle, loaded, horn) play over and over: steady rpm, no revving, gear change, voice or wind. Cut both ends at the same point of the engine cycle (ideally whole firing cycles), near a zero crossing, without fades, at an even level, so no click or pumping is heard on each lap.
  • Idle and low rpm: the module plays the loop up to 3x faster with the throttle, so record the engine idling; a loop recorded at high rpm sounds wrong at both ends.
  • Mid range, not bass: the 40 mm speaker plays nothing useful below about 200 Hz. The firing rhythm and the rasp above that carry the engine; a deep rumble alone sounds like silence.
  • Level: peaks at about -1 dB, no clipping, about as loud as the other sets.
  • Quiet background: noise gets louder and higher with the playback rate.
  • One-shots (starter, shift): no silence at the start, a short fade-out (about 50 ms) at the end.
  • Rights: your own recording or one you may use freely (for example CC0).
