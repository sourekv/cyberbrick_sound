# Shield rev A: ideal parts against the ordered ones

Generated by `tools/make_shield_bom_compare.py` from `hardware/shield/fab/shield_bom.csv` (the ideal, full BOM of the design) and `fab/jlcpcb_basic/shield_bom_jlcpcb.csv` (what was ordered at JLCPCB on 2026-11-13, order [order number removed]). The same table as CSV: `hardware/shield/fab/shield_bom_compare.csv`.

6 of 29 references differ, in three groups, all chosen to get JLCPCB's basic parts (about 9 USD less in loading fees):

- **C3, C6, C7**: Samsung CL10B104KB8NNNC -> YAGEO CC0603KRX7R9BB104: the same 100 nF 50 V X7R 0603, no effect
- **FB1, FB2**: Murata BLM18KG601SN1D (600 R, 1.3 A) -> BLM18PG121SN1D (120 R, 2 A): weaker filtering of the amplifier's switching noise on the speaker wires, lower resistance; the beads are optional in the design (can be 0 R)
- **LED1**: Kento KT-0603G green -> KT-0603R red: no green 0603 LED among JLCPCB's basic parts; the red one has a lower drop, so a little more than the 3 mA through the 1 k resistor (to be seen at the first power-up)

J1, J3 and J4 are not in JLCPCB's assembly and are soldered by hand; JP1 and JP2 are solder jumpers on the board, not parts.
