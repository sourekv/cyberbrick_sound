    The board is the size of the CyberBrick receiver shield (29 × 30 mm) with the same four mounting holes. The ESP32-S3-Zero sits in the middle; most parts are in the gap between its two socket strips, the power LED and the spare GPIO header are beside it so they stay visible. Footprints and heights are typical values, to be replaced by the real ones when the schematic is drawn. The same layout as the 2D mock (docs/shield-mock.png).
